## AI plus quantum chemistry compresses synthesis-route development

Source: NEDO (Japan)
Publication date: 2026-09-30
Original: https://www.nedo.go.jp/news/press/AA5_101968.html

NEDO, TS Technology and Nara Institute of Science and Technology report AMATERUS AI, a workflow combining several AI stages for synthesis-route search and prediction with quantum-chemical reaction evaluation and process design. In a pharmaceutical synthesis evaluation, they report reducing work from 64 person-weeks to 12 person-weeks, roughly one fifth of the conventional development effort. The underlying R&D programme ran from 2019 through 2025; a commercial service began on September 30.

Why it may matter: this is more interesting as a methodological signal than as an AI product launch. It combines learned search with physics-based calculation and process engineering across an actual experimental-development workflow, rather than asking a model to propose molecules in isolation. If the claimed compression generalizes beyond the demonstrated pharmaceutical case, it is evidence for a broader shift toward closed computational funnels that prune enormous experimental search spaces before wet-lab work.

Caution/follow-up: the 5x figure is reported by the programme participants, not an independent replication. Track independent users, performance on chemically diverse targets, and whether route novelty and experimental success survive outside the original project.
